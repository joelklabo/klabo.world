# Confirmed newspaper images of 28 Brown Court / Brown Court Electrical Home

I scanned the July 1924 capture set and the follow-up-page contact sheets for newspaper images of the house itself, inside or outside. This folder contains the clippings that appear to be actual house/room photos or the 2007 exterior newspaper photo.

Excluded from this confirmed-photo set:

- generic ads and unrelated news photos;
- furniture/product illustrations not actually showing the house;
- the 1924 dining-set illustration ad, which references the Model Electrical Home but appears to be an illustration rather than a house photo.

Files:

1. `02_1924-07-12_mrs-housewife_interior.jpg` — doorway/room image in the “Mrs. Housewife / The Electrical Home” ad.
2. `03_1924-07-14_monday-washday_interior.jpg` — interior/entry scene from “Monday isn’t washday at the Electrical Home.”
3. `05_1924-07-16_kitchen-of-your-dreams.jpg` — kitchen photo at the Electrical Home.
4. `06_1924-07-17_livable-room.jpg` — living room photo at the Electrical Home.
5. `07_1924-07-18_distinctive-room_argus.jpg` — dining room photo, Argus version.
6. `08_1924-07-18_dining-room_courier.jpg` — dining room photo, Courier version.
7. `09_1924-07-19_tomorrow-last-day_interior.jpg` — room/interior photo from final-day ad.
8. `10_2007_exterior_28-brown-court.jpg` — 2007 exterior newspaper photo of 28 Brown Court.
